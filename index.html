Learn AI PPT with Phoebe

Six sessions, 45 minutes each, and one real deck you build across all of them. AI can make a presentation in thirty seconds. It cannot tell you whether the room understood it. This course teaches the part that decides: the 30-second test, the one slide method with peer-reviewed evidence behind it, and an honest read on which presentation advice you have been handed is research and which is just repeated a lot.

The six sessions 🎬 · one real deck, built across all of them

Pick a deck you genuinely have to present in the next month. Not a practice deck. Every session moves that one forward, and the order is deliberate, because the mistakes compound backwards: people design slides before they have an argument, and then no amount of design saves it.

⏱️ Session 1 · easy

The 30-second test

Thirty seconds after your slide goes up, can the room say what you are asking for? Meet the test, watch a real slide climb from 27 percent understood to 91, and learn the method that has actual research behind it.

▶ Start here 🧱 Session 2 · easy

Outline first

Your deck is an argument. Generate the structure, then kill half of it. Why every good AI deck tool puts an editable outline gate in front of you, and why the bad ones skip straight to slides.

the argument 🎯 Session 3 · medium

Slides that carry

Assertion-evidence in full: a sentence headline that states the message, one visual that proves it. One idea per slide, one hero number, and the three Mayer principles that decide whether any of it lands.

biggest lever 📊 Session 4 · medium

Charts and data

Charts that argue instead of decorate, and where fabricated figures actually come from. Microsoft says Copilot is not optimised for verbatim reproduction of large inputs. That sentence is why your number drifted.

integrity 🛠️ Session 5 · hands-on

Build the deck

The real build, tool by tool, with the licence and export traps named: Copilot needs a Designer licence for half its features, Gamma flattens gradients, Canva warns your PPTX may not survive PowerPoint. Plus a course still selling a tool that no longer exists.

the real thing 🗣️ Session 6 · hands-on

The room

Speaker notes, the Q and A you are dreading, and the accessibility pass no AI tool will do for you. This is where the last nine points of the score live, and no vendor claims WCAG conformance for its output.
